What changed during installation?

I investigate three installation-related hypotheses, comparing their mechanisms with measurable evidence.

INVESTIGATION ORDER

Isolate and inspect → verify connections and protection → check supply under a controlled start → separate electrical and mechanical causes.

Break the interference path.

EMI is the interference. EMC is the ability of the systems to operate together without unacceptable interference. RF communication is not required for switching noise to cause problems.

03 / VERIFY

A design becomes credible through tests.

A

Traction works

I compare battery and controller input voltage during controlled starts, alongside current, controller fault state and drivetrain behaviour.

Evidence:

Starts under representative load without unexplained protection trips; voltage and current stay within confirmed equipment ratings.

B

People are protected

I inspect bond terminations, measure protective bonding continuity, assess insulation and test the designed fault-response chain.

Evidence:

Bonding and insulation satisfy the agreed design criteria; a simulated monitored fault safely removes traction enable.

C

Communication survives

I compare communication with traction off, during starts and under changing motor demand.

Evidence:

No unacceptable message errors, resets or loss of communication, evaluated through logs and measurements.
