Why starting is different from running

A stationary DC motor has little or no back EMF: the voltage generated by rotation that opposes the supply. Starting current can therefore be high, unless the controller limits it. For a fixed-flux DC motor, torque is approximately proportional to armature current. Speed also depends on applied voltage, load and losses. “Current gives torque; voltage affects speed” is a useful starting rule, not a complete motor model.

A controller commonly uses PWM—rapid switching to vary average applied voltage—but the interview file does not specify Wallaby’s exact controller or PWM implementation. V = IR explains a voltage drop across a resistance; it does not by itself describe the entire motor.

Grounding assumption: floating traction supply.

Workers servicing the equipment, onboard operators and passengers may touch exposed metal. This candidate architecture keeps both DC poles insulated from the chassis, bonds exposed metal together, and uses insulation monitoring with a defined shutdown response. Confirm the real architecture and applicable requirements with MREx before implementation. If the traction supply is intentionally chassis-referenced, the fault-current path and automatic disconnection must instead be designed and verified for that arrangement. A fuse alone may not clear a first insulation fault on a floating system.

Shielding and interface considerations

At high frequencies, a low-impedance shield connection to the enclosure at cable entry is often important. A long thin shield pigtail can be inductive and ineffective. Bonding shields at both ends can help at high frequency where the chassis bonding architecture supports it, but low-frequency potential differences and circulating currents must also be assessed. The design depends on the cable, interface and vehicle grounding arrangement.

Twisted pair is most effective when the interface uses a compatible balanced or differential signalling method. The document says only that data is sent by cable; it does not identify CAN, RS-485 or another protocol. Ferrites, common-mode chokes, filters, transient suppression and isolated interfaces are options to select from measured noise and equipment requirements, rather than additions to make indiscriminately. If cables must cross, crossing approximately at right angles can reduce the length of close parallel coupling.

03 / VERIFY

A design becomes credible through tests.

A

Traction works

Compare battery and controller input voltage during controlled starts. Check current, controller fault state and drivetrain behaviour.

Evidence:

Starts under representative load without unexplained protection trips; voltage and current stay within confirmed equipment ratings.

B

People are protected

Inspect bond terminations; measure protective bonding continuity; assess insulation and test the designed fault-response chain.

Evidence:

Bonding and insulation satisfy the agreed design criteria; a simulated monitored fault safely removes traction enable.

C

Communication survives

Compare communication behaviour with traction off and during starts, changing motor demand and representative operation.

Evidence:

No unacceptable message errors, resets or loss of communication; confirm with logs and suitable measurements.

Before a powered test

Agree the procedure with the team: isolate for inspection, secure the locomotive, control wheel movement, use correctly rated measurement equipment and reconnect under supervision. Never measure resistance on an energised circuit. Work on exposed live circuitry belongs in an approved test procedure.

Questions I would bring to the electrical team

What are the battery voltage, startup current and controller limits?
Needed to select conductor, fuse, contactor, protection and measurement ratings.

Is traction floating or chassis-referenced?
Determines insulation monitoring, fault-current paths and automatic shutdown.

Which communication interface and cable are used?
Determines transceiver, impedance termination, shielding and isolation choices.

What electrical and competition requirements apply?
Needed to establish bond resistance, insulation and acceptance criteria.
